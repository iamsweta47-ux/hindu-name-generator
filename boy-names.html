About Hindu boy names

Hindu boy names are traditionally chosen from Sanskrit and Hindi roots, often carrying meanings connected to nature, virtues, or the names and qualities of deities. A name like Aarav means "peaceful," while Veer means "brave" — parents often choose names that reflect the qualities they hope their child will carry through life.

Tips for choosing a name

  • Say the name out loud with your family surname to check the flow.
  • Consider the meaning carefully — many Hindu names carry a specific virtue or blessing.
  • Check the name against your family's Rashi or Nakshatra naming tradition, if you follow one.
  • Shortlist a few favorites and use the copy button to share them with family for feedback.
