About Hindu Name Generator

A simple, free tool built to help families find names rich with meaning.

Our story

Hindu Name Generator started as a small project to make one part of preparing for a new baby a little easier: finding a name that feels right and carries real meaning. Sanskrit and Hindi naming traditions are deep and beautiful, but sifting through long lists in books or scattered websites can be overwhelming. We built this site to make that search fast, clear, and enjoyable.

What we offer

Our generators cover Hindu baby boy names and Hindu baby girl names, each with a short and accurate meaning drawn from Sanskrit and Hindi roots. You can filter by starting letter, choose how many names to see, and copy your favorites with a single tap. We also built a couple name combiner for blending two names into a ship name, matching initials, and cute nicknames — a fun tool for engagements, weddings, or just for fun.

Our approach

We keep the site fast, simple, and free of clutter. There's no sign-up, no account, and no unnecessary tracking. Advertising is limited to a few clearly labeled, non-intrusive placements so the site can stay free for everyone.
